# PivotAI 🚀

### AI-Powered Career Discovery & Job Matching Agent

**PivotAI** is an AI-powered career discovery platform designed to connect people with relevant job opportunities based on their skills, experience, projects, and interests — regardless of their academic background.

Instead of limiting job recommendations to a candidate's degree or current profession, PivotAI aims to understand what a person can actually do and discover opportunities across different roles and industries.

> Your skills define your possibilities, not just your degree.

## 🎯 Problem Statement

Traditional job searches often rely heavily on job titles, degrees, and predefined career paths. This can make it difficult for candidates to discover opportunities that match their actual capabilities, particularly when their skills extend beyond their academic background or previous experience.

PivotAI aims to make job discovery more skill-centric, personalised, and explainable.

## ✨ Planned Features

- **Skill-Based Job Discovery:** Discover relevant opportunities based on skills, projects, experience, and interests.
- **Intelligent Job Matching:** Retrieve and rank job opportunities according to candidate profiles and job requirements.
- **Explainable Recommendations:** Understand why a job is recommended and which candidate skills support the match.
- **Skill-Gap Analysis:** Identify missing skills and distinguish essential requirements from preferred qualifications.
- **Transferable Skill Identification:** Discover how existing skills and experience can apply to different roles and industries.
- **Personalised Career Roadmaps:** Suggest learning priorities and projects to help candidates become suitable for target roles.
- **Conversational AI Assistant:** Interact with an AI agent to explore opportunities, refine preferences, and ask career-related questions.

## 🧠 Technical Direction

The project is intended to explore and implement:

- Large Language Models (LLMs) and structured outputs
- Resume parsing and structured candidate profiles
- Text embeddings and semantic search
- Hybrid information retrieval
- Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG)
- Ranking and recommendation systems
- Tool-calling and agentic workflows
- Evaluation of retrieval quality and LLM-generated recommendations
- Backend API development and database integration
